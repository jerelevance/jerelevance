The Strength of Weak Ties, in Phil Chat: week of Oct 21

Granovetter argued that the ties holding a network together are rarely the close ones. Inside a clique everyone already knows what everyone knows; new information crosses between cliques on weak ties. This is the debate channel's latest five days drawn his way: six groups, the strong and weak ties inside them, and the weak-tie bridges between them.

    Where the map departs from Granovetter

    Granovetter predicted that no strong tie is a bridge. In the last log, five people carried strong ties into several groups at once. This week the switchboard narrowed. Only 12 ties count as strong, and just four of them cross a group line: Jere to Hugh, Angel and SuperVillainy, and aura to Hugh. aura, Hugh and Jere sit on the most shortest paths between chatters, roughly twice as many as anyone else. Everything else that crosses between groups, 72 ties, is weak.

    The time part of Granovetter's definition shows up clearly. Jambe and Ixtlimal traded 120 messages in a single overnight session on Oct 24, about the gnostics and St Paul, and never spoke again, so their tie counts as weak despite its volume. SuparB and Zone are the same: 65 exchanges in one afternoon on Oct 26. Volume without repetition does not make a strong tie. aura and Jere, who talked on five of the six days, do.

    Versus talk was mostly kept inside groups this week. SuperVillainy and Zone remained the strongest pair in their group after the lying-with-true-information fight, then went silent after Oct 24. Hugh and hpaigecr, whose tie collapsed in the last log, said nothing to each other until Oct 26, when they agreed that LLM concepts are "constituted differently" and fought anyway. Only one bridge is versus (hpaigecr and SuparB). Three are mixed, and in two of them (bossOnothin with SuperVillainy, aura with Angel) the friction turned into agreement within the same conversation.

    How this was measured

    The source is the debate channel export from Oct 21, 23:17 UTC to Oct 27, 01:31 UTC (5,958 messages). Ties come from direct replies (weight 1) plus quick back-and-forth, meaning messages from two people within two minutes of each other with no reply marker (weight 0.25). Following Granovetter's mix of time, intensity and reciprocity, a tie's strength combines its volume, how many days it appeared, how many of the three stretches it was active in, and how even the replies were in both directions. The top tenth of ties count as strong; all other ties with at least 4 exchanges count as weak.

    Only the 33 people with 30 or more messages are drawn. The moderator, Pearl, is left out. Groups come from community detection on the weighted reply network. Briefs and the catnip and versus judgments come from reading the exchanges, so they are calls, not counts. Health details that came up in chat are left out of the briefs.
