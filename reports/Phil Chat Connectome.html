Same word, different wiring

Most of the chat's fights aren't really about a claim. Two people use the same word, but in each head it fires a different chain and lands on a different feeling. Each one hears the other denying something obvious.

When Hugh says “AI understands,” Jere's [AI] chain hears “appearance is reality.” When Jere says “it's just tokens,” Hugh's chain hears “dismissal reflex.” Neither is responding to the other's actual wiring.
