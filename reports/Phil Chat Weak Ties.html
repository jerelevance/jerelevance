The strength of weak ties, in Phil Chat

Granovetter drew groups of strong ties joined by weak-tie bridges. This is the same picture for the 131 accounts in the debate channel: eight crews, 74 strong ties, 235 weak ones, and three true local bridges.

Weak ties do the crossing

185 of the 230 ties between crews are weak. Inside crews, 50 of 79 are. The 45 strong ties that do cross crews mostly run through the hubs: Hugh is on 12 of them and Jere on 10.

Strong ties share friends

A strong tie has a median of 12 mutual contacts and a weak tie has 6. Across all 309 ties, weight and mutual contacts have a rank correlation of 0.47, the direction Granovetter predicted.

Strict bridges are rare

Only three ties join two crews with no mutual contact and a shortest alternate route of three steps. All three are weak (weights 9 to 28). FenceJumper is on two of them. Moving the tie cutoff anywhere from 3 to 25 leaves between 2 and 9 such ties.

The forbidden triad is not forbidden

Granovetter expects that if A is strongly tied to B and to C, then B and C at least know each other. Here 146 of 495 such pairs have no tie even at the weak cutoff. Hugh accounts for 52 of them: he talks one-on-one with people who never talk to each other.

How the ties are drawn

Tie weight
Replies, plus 1.5 for each @mention, plus 0.35 for each back-to-back exchange, between the same two accounts. Youv and Youvious are merged.
Weak tie
Weight of 8 or more, roughly eight replies' worth. 309 pairs clear it.
Strong tie
Weight of 40 or more, with at least three replies in each direction. 74 pairs clear it. Granovetter's strength mixes time, intensity, intimacy and reciprocity. This uses volume and reciprocity, the only parts a chat log shows.
Crews
The eight groups from the Social Analysis, found by repeated clustering of the reply map. Accounts outside the 47 tiered chatters join the crew of the person they interacted with most.
Local bridge
A weak tie between two crews whose ends share no mutual contact, so the shortest alternate route is three steps or more. Degree is that route's length. Ties to an account with no other tie are left out, since a dangling account bridges nothing.
Near-bridge
A weak tie between two crews whose ends share exactly one mutual contact. This is a looser class added here, not Granovetter's term.
Limits
One channel over 24 days, and replies are a thin stand-in for closeness. Crews are loose: 230 of the 309 ties cross a crew line, so these are looser clusters than Granovetter's cliques. Tie weights come from the export alone, so anything said in voice or elsewhere is invisible.
